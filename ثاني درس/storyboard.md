# Storyboard — Lesson 02: Cinematic Anatomical Atlas

## Chapter 00 — Hero: The Reveal
- **Visual**: Dark Obsidian room. Human anatomical silhouette gradually illuminates under X-Ray Cyan scanning laser.
- **Focus Order**: Skull Glow → Vertebral Column Glow → Rib Cage Glow → Limb outlines (muted bone-shadow tone, since Chapters 06-07 now cover them in depth).
- **Text**: "الهيكل العظمي الكامل في الإنسان" | "Axial & Appendicular Skeleton — 206 عظمة تحمي وتحرك جسمك"
- **Action**: CTA Button "ابدأ الرحلة التشريحية" smooth scrolls to Chapter 01.

## Chapter 01 — System: Skeleton vs Skeletal System
- **Concept**: Difference between Bone Skeleton (206 bones) and Complete Skeletal System (Bones + Cartilage + Joints + Ligaments + Tendons = 5 components).
- **Interactive Visual**: Assembly view showing 206 bones connecting with cartilages, joints, ligaments, tendons.

## Chapter 02 — Axial vs Appendicular
- **Concept**: Central Axis (80 bones: Skull + Spine + Rib Cage) vs Appendicular (126 bones: Girdles + Limbs).
- **Interactive Visual**: Split toggle 1 vs 2 (Single central axis vs paired appendages: Skull 1, Column 1, Cage 1 vs Arms 2, Legs 2).

## Chapter 03 — Vertebral Column (Hero Anatomy)
- **Concept**: Main axis, 33 vertebrae (7 Cervical, 12 Thoracic, 5 Lumbar, 5 Sacral fused, 4 Coccygeal fused = 26 bones).
- **Interactive Zoom & Cross Section**: Hollow interior ("مقور من الداخل") -> Neural Canal housing Crimson Spinal Cord.

## Chapter 04 — Skull: The Protective Box
- **Concept**: Cranial part (8 bones with serrated joints) & Facial part (14 bones). Houses brain and sense organs.
- **Visual Metaphor**: Solid protective box surrounding and protecting delicate brain.

## Chapter 05 — Thoracic Cage & Respiration Simulation
- **Concept**: 37 bones (12 Thoracic Vertebrae + 24 Ribs + 1 Sternum).
- **Interactive Simulation**: Breathing toggle (Inspiration/الشهيق: Ribs move outwards and forwards; Expiration/الزفير: Ribs move inwards and backwards).

## Chapter 06 — Pectoral Girdle & Upper Limb
- **Concept**: Pectoral Girdle (4 bones: Clavicle x2 + Scapula x2) — the only direct bony link between the upper limb and the axial skeleton (sternoclavicular joint); scapula itself floats on muscle only. Free Upper Limb (30 bones per side / 60 total): Humerus 1, Ulna & Radius 2, Carpals 8, Metacarpals 5, Phalanges 14.
- **Interactive Visual**: Schematic SVG of clavicle, scapula (with glenoid cavity), humerus, and forearm bones.

## Chapter 07 — Pelvic Girdle & Lower Limb
- **Concept**: Pelvic Girdle (2 bones, each fused from Ilium + Ischium + Pubis) — firmly joined to the sacrum via the sacroiliac joint. Free Lower Limb (30 bones per side / 60 total): Femur 1, Patella 1, Tibia & Fibula 2, Tarsals 7, Metatarsals 5, Phalanges 14.
- **Interactive Visual**: Schematic SVG of the pelvic girdle, femur, patella, tibia and fibula.
- **Closing beat**: Completion callout — `4 + 60 + 2 + 60 = 126` appendicular bones, `126 + 80 = 206` total, tying back to Chapter 01's headline number.

## Chapter 08 — Direct vs Indirect Connections
- **Concept**: How skeleton parts anchor to the vertebral column.
- **Visual Grid**: Direct (Skull, Thoracic Cage) vs Indirect (Pectoral Girdle -> Upper Limbs, Pelvic Girdle -> Lower Limbs).

## Chapter 09 — Exam Traps (Mokhtabar Al-Akhta'a)
- **Visual**: Red Flag High Alert cards.
- **Traps**:
  1. Pelvic Girdle is in the center but belongs to APPENDICULAR (2 bones).
  2. Ligaments (Bone-Bone) vs Tendons (Muscle-Bone).
  3. Skeletal System (5 components) vs Skeleton (206 bones).
  4. Pelvis (hip bones + sacrum + coccyx) vs Pelvic Girdle (hip bones only).
  5. The Patella is a commonly-forgotten sesamoid bone within the 206 count.
